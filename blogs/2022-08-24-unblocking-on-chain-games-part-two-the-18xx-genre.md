Francis Tresham was a brilliant, if oft forgotten, designer who pioneered the 4X genre and introduced the game pattern “technology trees.” Sid Meier’s Civilization (1991), despite its auteurist title, was actually an adaptation of Tresham’s board game, Civilization (1980).
